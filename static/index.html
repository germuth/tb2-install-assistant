🧗 Tension Board 2 Installation Tips & Tricks

1. Order of Operations: Plastic First ("Set-by-Set" / Bag-by-Bag), Then Wood

Install all 137 plastic holds first—their distinct shapes act as landmarks for placing the 130 wood holds. In Tension's manual, "set-by-set" refers to board crop sets (10x8 Base vs. 12x8/12x12 Expansion boxes). Since 10x8 is the base crop, every hold in your plastic boxes belongs on your wall (split across 2 boxes for shipping weight, grouped in inner bags). You can either follow Guided Mode bottom-up, or open one bag at a time and press / to type the stamped Hold ID.

2. Wood Hold Orientation Rule

Wood holds have an orientation arrow/SKU stamped directly on the hold. The arrow is oriented perpendicular to the screw holes, NOT aligned with the usable grip surface. Always orient the stamped arrow toward the nautical direction indicated in the app.

3. Official 16-Point Nautical Compass Guide

Directions like E/SE (ESE) or W/SW (WSW) sit halfway (22.5°) between the cardinal and 45° diagonal lines. Align the hold's molded/stamped reference arrow from the center hole toward that compass heading:

4. Impact Driver Torque Clutch

Adjust the torque/speed clutch on your impact driver. Drive screws until the hold is completely flush with no rocking. Do NOT over-torque, which can crack wood holds or countersink too deep.

5. Keyboard Shortcuts for Hands-Free Speed
  • Space / Enter: Drill in & mark installed (advances to next hold)
  • Right Arrow: Skip to next hold
  • Left Arrow: Go back to previous hold
  • U: Undo / toggle installed status
  • / or F: Focus hold search box

📦 Split Plastic Holds: Box 1 vs. Box 2

Tension packs the 10×8 Plastic set into 2 cartons by weight without a fixed public Box 1 vs. Box 2 manifest (holds are grouped inside clear inner bags by ID / L+R pairs). Open Box 1, tap or type the Hold IDs on its bags below, and keep Box 2 sealed—everything untagged automatically stays in Box 2!
